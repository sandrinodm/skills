# A smart speaker listens on the device, and audio leaves only after the wake word

## Request

Draw what's inside our smart speaker for the privacy page: the parts on the device, the privacy gate, and the cloud services audio can reach.

## Brief

Title: A smart speaker listens on the device, and audio leaves only after the wake word
Subtitle: What stays on the speaker and what reaches the cloud
Pattern: Anatomy
Theme: quiet
Accent: Privacy gate

- You, anywhere in the room: talks to the speaker
- On the speaker itself (container): Microphones, Wake-word detector, Local controls, Speaker, Short buffer, Settings
- Privacy gate (accent band): audio leaves only after the wake word
- Voice assistant, Music services, Smart home: reached only through the gate

Layout: the person on top, the speaker as a container with the parts in 2 rows of 3, the gate as a full-width band at the bottom, the three services below it.
